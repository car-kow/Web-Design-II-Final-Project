Cat Vaccinations

    FVRCP

  • Protects against three common infectious dieseases whose symptoms range from severe to fatal.

    • Rabies

  • Legally required for all cats to prevent fatalities in both animals and humans.

    • Feline Leukemia

  • One of the leading causes of death in pet cats, with annual bosters being essential.

    • FIV

  • Most common in outdoor cats that get into fights, the best way to protect your cat is to keep them indoors while vaccinations are slowly becoming accepted.

Dog Vaccinations

    DAP-P

  • Protects against four common infectious diseases, with regular boosters being required.

    • Rabies

  • Legally required for all dogs to prevent fatalities in both animals and humans.

    • Bordetella

  • Prevents "kennel cough", required for admission to most day cares and training organizations.

    • Lepto

  • Protects against common varities of leptospirosis, which can cause liver and kidney damage to humans.
