Vaccinations

Feline Vaccines

  • FVRCP vaccines protect against three common infectious dieseases whose symptoms range from severe to fatal.
  • Rabies vaccines are legally required for all cats to prevent fatalities in both animals and humans.
  • Feline Leukemia vaccines protect against one of the leading causes of death in pet cats, with annual bosters being essential.
  • FIV vaccines are reccomended for outdoor cats that get into fights. The best way to protect your cat is to keep them indoors while vaccinations are slowly becoming accepted.

Canine Vaccines

  • DAP-P vaccines protect against four common infectious diseases, with regular boosters being required.
  • Rabies vaccines are legally required for all dogs to prevent fatalities in both animals and humans.
  • Bordetella vaccines protect against "kennel cough", and are required for admission to most day cares and training organizations.
  • Lepto vaccines protect against common varities of leptospirosis, which can spread from animals to cause liver and kidney damage in humans.
