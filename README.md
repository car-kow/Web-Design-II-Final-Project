# Web Design II Final Project - Beechmont Pet Hospital Website Redesign

## Creator

Carter Kowalak

## Description

I am redesigning the website for a local pet hospital. The current header is cluttered in sections, there are multiple separate pages that can be consolidated into a singular page, and there are duplicate social media links in the footer which are not functional.

The site will be redesigned to prioritize the ability for a wide range of beginner web users across both desktop and mobile to schedule appointments, understand the services provided by the hospital, find medical information regarding common issues, and find information about the staff to establish the website as professional and trustworthy.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** Individuals or families looking to schedule medical appoinments for their pets. 
  
- **Secondary:** Individuals seeking trustworthy medical information for taking care of their pets, as well as those looking for job opportunities and internships in the field of veterinary medicine.

## Project Goals

- Simplify the header and make options to schedule more clearly marked and organized
- Consolidate pages with a single paragraph of text into one singular page for multiple pieces of information
- Organize services according to animal, rather than having them unorganized
- Remove the custom paw mouse icon that activates while using the website
